Our Mission Our mission is to help create a world where, within our lifetime, we see meaningful and lasting change in the way humans treat animals—with greater responsibility, compassion, empathy, and respect. We strive not only to transform the lives of animals, but also to transform the way people understand the human-animal relationship, recognizing that the well-being of animals and a healthy, respectful relationship between humans and animals are essential to building a more compassionate and responsible society.

About us

Veterinary care for the animals who have no one.

Nina To The Rescue is a non-profit organization focused on providing veterinary care to animals in need in El Salvador. We work where care is hardest to reach: free and low-cost surgery, vaccination and treatment for street animals and for families who cannot afford a clinic.

Our mission

To help create a world where, within our lifetime, we see meaningful and lasting change in the way humans treat animals — with greater responsibility, compassion, empathy and respect.

We strive not only to transform the lives of animals, but also to transform the way people understand the human-animal relationship, recognizing that the well-being of animals and a healthy, respectful relationship between humans and animals are essential to building a more compassionate and responsible society.

"

Meet our lead veterinarian

Dr. Jeff Young

We are honored to welcome Dr. Jeff Young, widely known as "Dr. Jeff: Rocky Mountain Vet," as the lead veterinarian for our first campaign in El Salvador. With decades dedicated to veterinary medicine, accessible care, spay and neuter initiatives and animal welfare, Dr. Jeff has inspired people around the world through his commitment to improving the lives of animals.

Our El Salvador campaign, "Dr. Jeff por el Bienestar Animal," is named in recognition of his extraordinary dedication and decades of service to animals and the communities that care for them. We are deeply honored to have him lead this important first chapter of our mission.
